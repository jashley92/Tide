# What's new in v1.14.0

**Reports** has tabs, and a story can start an article.

## Analysts

- **Add an article from a story.** On a story's page, *New article on this story* starts a draft with the story's CVEs, tags and products we run. *Add an existing article* puts one you've written on the story.
- **Your brief timeliness.** Reports › *Brief timeliness* shows your own brief days: when both editions went, when the SharePoint copy was posted, and whether each was on time.

## Presenters

- **Who attended** shows on a brief only once its meeting has been held.
- **In rehearsal**, the arrow keys move the slides while a step of *Learn to present* is showing.

## Administrators

- **A Manager role.** Map an AD group to it on **Role mappings** (Access & distribution). A Manager sees everyone's brief timeliness and can excuse a late day, with a reason. So can you.
- **Sent by and Posted by.** When the editions are due to be sent (11:30) and the SharePoint copy posted (12:00) are on **Organization**.
- **Reports has tabs**: Monthly report, Team commitments and Brief timeliness.

## Viewers and action owners

- **Team commitments by person.** On Reports › *Team commitments*, *By person* beside a team shows a line per person: your own, or everyone's for a Manager.
- **Managers** see every analyst's brief timeliness on Reports, and can excuse a late day the analyst warned of.
