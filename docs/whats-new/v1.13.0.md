# What's new in v1.13.0

Most pages have a new layout. The biggest change is the **context rail**.

## Analysts

- **The context rail.** Select anything (an article, an action, a feed item) and the column on the right shows what TIDE knows about it. Press `]` to show or hide it.
- **Guides.** Help has a short guide for each page, and *Show me around* on a page walks you through it.
- **Triage** opens a reading pane beside the list. It fetches an article only when you press `V`.
- **The editor** is one column in the order the brief reads, and saves as you go.
- **Take it** and assigning are now for members of the action's team.

## Presenters

- Your controls are in a **presenter's rail** that followers never see. Rehearse first with *Learn to present* in Help.

## Administrators

- New meeting and feed settings are in **Organization** and **Sources**.

## Viewers and action owners

- **The context rail.** Select anything (an article, an action, a feed item) and the column on the right shows what TIDE knows about it. Press `]` to show or hide it.
- **Guides.** Help has a short guide for each page, and *Show me around* on a page walks you through it.
