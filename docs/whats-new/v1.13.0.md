# What's new in v1.13.0

Most pages are laid out afresh. What you select now shows what TIDE knows about it, in a column on the right.

## Analysts

- **Every page.** The **context rail** on the right shows what TIDE knows about what you selected. Press `]` to show or hide it. On a narrower window, the rail button in the top bar opens it.
- **Top bar.** The brief chip says when the brief meets and which step it's on. Click it to go back to Today.
- **Today.** The brief takes the page. The edition check, your teams' queue, reviews and the meeting are in the rail. Beside a *Why it matters* warning, **Keep it** says the paragraph is meant for everyone as it is.
- **Triage.** The reading pane opens by itself on a wide window. It fetches an article only when you press `V` or *Read it here*. The decisions sit under it, with a line saying why the suggested step is suggested.
- **Triage groups.** A report an article already covers is attached in one click, and a covered group with **Attach all**.
- **Pull all.** On Triage's Sources tab, **Pull all** pulls every source, one at a time. Today says when feeds haven't been pulled since the morning.
- **The article editor.** One column, in the order the brief reads. The reader preview is in the rail. It saves itself after a short pause, but never a marking change.
- **Actions.** Each tab shows its count, and **No owner** finds work nobody has. Select a row with `J` and `K`; `T` takes it and `Enter` opens it.
- **Take it and assignees.** Take it is offered only on actions of a team you're on, and an action is assigned only to a member of its team. Ask an administrator to add you to a team whose actions you take on.
- **An action's page.** One column of work. The facts, the article and Files are in the rail. Drop a file anywhere on the page.
- **Search.** A lookup answers in its first line. The hunt query and the export are in the rail.
- **Our stack and the Library.** Products that need a look come first. Saved queries, templates, playbooks and board rules are one Library with four tabs.

## Presenters

- **Present mode.** Who is here and whose hand is up are in the top bar. Your controls are in the presenter's rail, never on a follower's screen.
- **Slides.** The article slide shows its indicators, and each recommended line the action it became. The wrap-up is three lists: not raised, who has what, and not discussed.
- **Actions that need review.** The meeting's open-actions slide now lists overdue and escalated actions, and open ones nobody has updated for a while.
- **Learn to present.** In Help, a rehearsal of present mode on today's brief, with a short guide. No meeting starts, and nothing is shared or saved.

## Administrators

- **Actions that need review.** In Organization settings, choose how many business days an open or a blocked action may go without an update before the meeting lists it.
- **An action beside the slide.** Also in Organization settings, and off until set: in a meeting, an action opens beside the slide. Try it in the meeting room first.
- **Feeds pulled by.** Today tells analysts how many feeds haven't been pulled since the time set in Sources (`Intake:PulledBy`).
- **Help** lists every release's full notes, the upgrade steps and new settings included.

## Viewers and action owners

- **Every page.** The **context rail** on the right shows what TIDE knows about what you selected. Press `]` to show or hide it. On a narrower window, the rail button in the top bar opens it.
- **Actions.** Each tab shows its count. Select a row with `J` and `K` and press `Enter` to open it. The rail shows its next step, its article and its last activity. Action owners take one of their own team's actions with **Take it**.
- **An action's page.** One column of work. The facts, the article and Files are in the rail.
- **An external team's action** that nobody here holds is named by its ticket, or "no ticket yet".
- **Search.** A lookup answers in its first line, and lists each article once.
- **In a meeting.** Who is here and whose hand is up are in the top bar.
