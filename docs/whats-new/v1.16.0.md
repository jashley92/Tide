# What's new in v1.16.0

**Triage** lets you decide several items at once and keep going after a promote, and every list can **save views**.

## Analysts

- **Promote and keep triaging.** `Shift+Enter` promotes and moves to the next item, with *Undo* (`Alt+Z`) and *Add to today's brief*.
- **Choose several items.** In Triage, `Space` and `Shift+J`/`K` choose items to promote as one, shortlist, dismiss or attach. New KEV items can become a *KEV round-up* draft.
- **Saved views** on Triage, Articles and Search, as on Actions.
- **Drafts in progress** show on Today. *Hand to…* in the editor gives a draft to a colleague with a note.
- **Articles: Mine and By.** *Mine* is what you're writing; *By…* picks an author.
- **Editor keys.** `G` then a letter jumps to a section, `N` goes to the next gap, and `Ctrl+Shift+Enter` marks the article ready. Review and History are one line each.
- **Ctrl+K runs the page's commands.** Type `>` to list only those.
- **Report assessments are saved** for their period, with a TLP and who edited them. Trends and Reports say what moved.

## Presenters

- **Send both editions** from one confirmation after the meeting.
- **Meeting notes become follow-ups.** *Raise as action* on a note, and an ended meeting shows a recap.
- **The meeting clock** shows time against how long the meeting takes.

## Administrators

- **Diagnostics** has *Performance* and *Data & retention* panels, and `/health/jobs` warns of a nearly full disk.
- **New options, off until you set them:** audit log archive, purging removed files' bytes, a stale-backup warning, OTLP metrics and a certificate-protected key ring. See the upgrade notes.
- **After upgrading,** re-run `02-Grant-Backup.sql` so backups record their runs.

## Viewers and action owners

- **Act on several actions at once** on Actions: take, assign, move, set a due date or hand off.
- **Ctrl+K runs the page's commands.** On an action: *Take*, *Hand to…* and *Copy the hunt query*.
