# What's new in v1.16.0

**Triage** decides several items at once, lists can **save views**, and the theme can follow your system.

## Analysts

- **Triage.** `Shift+Enter` promotes and moves on, with *Undo*. `Space` chooses several items to decide together.
- **Saved views** on Triage, Articles and Search, and *Hand to…* gives a draft to a colleague.
- **Outreach templates** leave out what's empty, and the editor offers placeholders as you type `{{`.
- **Ctrl+K runs the page's commands.** Type `>` to list only those.

## Presenters

- **Send both editions** at once, and meeting notes can become actions.

## Administrators

- **Diagnostics** has *Performance* and *Data & retention* panels.
- **After upgrading,** re-run `02-Grant-Backup.sql`. New options stay off until you set them: see the upgrade notes.

## Viewers and action owners

- **Act on several actions at once** on Actions.
- **Outreach templates** leave out what's empty, and the editor offers placeholders as you type `{{`.
