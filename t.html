Counted by due date: an action due in the window is on time when it was done by that day, and late when it was done after it or is still open past it. Cancelled and not applicable ones are neither, nor is one due today and still open. Days late are calendar days. Open and overdue are as of today, whatever the window. On time vs. original date judges the actions first due in the window by the date they were first given, so moving a date doesn't turn a missed commitment into a kept one; Dates moved is how many of them had their date moved, and how often.
